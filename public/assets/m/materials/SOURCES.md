# M material assets

Local derivatives of existing Poly Haven CC0 assets: oak_wood_planks, fabric_pattern_07 and kloofendal_48d_partly_cloudy_puresky. Original provenance remains in the Blender assets and G400 material sources. The daylight HDR is resized to 1024 × 512; normal and roughness maps are linear data. No external requests are made by the visitor.
